## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary:** Technical recruiters and potential freelance clients looking for high-end Fullstack/AI expertise.
- **Context:** Browsing for talent that stands out from the "standard" pool. They need to see immediate technical competence and attention to detail.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice:** Cybernetic, Precise, Professional.
- **Tone:** High-tech, reliable, and slightly futuristic. It should feel like a premium terminal or a mission control center.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone:** Cyberpunk-adjacent but refined (not "neon-drenched"). High-contrast dark mode by default, with a sophisticated light mode toggle.
- **Typography:** Moving away from standard sans-serifs. Orbitron for headers (used precisely) paired with a high-quality, less common body face.
- **Micro-interactions:** Every interaction should feel mechanical and intentional—staggered reveals, scanning effects, and subtle grid-based motion.

### Design Principles
1. **Mechanical Precision:** Use grids, borders, and monospaced elements to imply structure and engineering rigor.
2. **Intentional Motion:** No "bounce" or "float." Use staggered reveals and linear-to-exponential transitions that feel like a system booting up.
3. **Data-Rich but Clean:** Show information through UI patterns (scanning lines, coordinate systems) rather than just blocks of text.
4. **Interactive Reliability:** Hover states and transitions must be snappy and provide clear feedback, reinforcing the "Professional" pillar.
